acidic and basic conditions. The addition of hydroxy groups to the aldehyde or amine monomers can

but COF-4 did not. COF-4 contains a different type of C=N bonds, and elemental analysis revealed a loss
of six additional hydrogen atoms per repeat unit relative to COF-3 as the only change. COF-5 irreversibly
isomerises to COF-6, which did not contain imine stretches in its IR spectrum.
