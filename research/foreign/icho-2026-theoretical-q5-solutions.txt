5.5
Which lipid phase is formed by PL1 when both acid residues in it are protonated?
Note that knowledge of PL1 structure is not required to solve this question.
Tick the correct answer.
(a) micellar
(b) lamellar
(c) hexagonal
(d) inverse
hexagonal
SOLUTION:
PL1 has 4 acyl chains but only 2 phosphate head groups, so its tail volume is
already large relative to the head group — which is why the dianion is lamellar,
not micellar/hexagonal. Protonating both acidic groups shrinks the head group
further (loss of charge and its water shell), tipping the balance even more to-
ward the tails. Starting from lamellar, this shift leads to the inverse hexagonal
phase. Correct answer is (d).
Only for correct answer: 2 point.
