Part C. Porphyrin sandwiches
Porphyrin sandwich-type complexes form when there are several porphyrin rings aligned relative to each
other. Examples are Zr(OEP)2, Eu2(OEP)3, Bi2(OEP)2Cl2 that have at least 3 planes of symmetry each.
Note that the hole size in the OEP ligand is ca. 4.0 Å, and the average metal–N distances in these com-
plexes are 2.4, 2.5, 2.3 Å, respectively.
