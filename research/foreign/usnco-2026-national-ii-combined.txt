together in the crystal.  Molecules with more regular shapes (such as the zigzag-shaped

surface area.  The two isomers are both tube shaped and have the same surface area and 
thus very similar boiling points.  (A branched isomer, such as 2-methyl-1-butene, has a 
smaller surface area and thus weaker intermolecular forces and a correspondingly lower
